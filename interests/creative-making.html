Why I love it

I like the moment when a rough idea becomes something concrete: a small tool, a layout, a diagram that makes a complicated idea clear. This portfolio is one example, designed and built to feel like the night scenes I enjoy.

What it teaches me

Clear design is a form of analysis. It means deciding what matters, removing what does not and presenting the rest so a reader understands it quickly. That is also the core of good data visualisation and reporting, which is why I see my creative side and my analytical side as the same skill.
