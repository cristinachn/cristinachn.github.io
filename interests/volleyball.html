How it started

I started playing volleyball at 12. My friends and I played at every school break, and it was the part of the day I looked forward to most. I decided to try it seriously in a club, and I soon found out that I had a talent for it.

Becoming a middle blocker

As we moved up to higher levels, the coaches began assigning positions, and I ended up as a middle blocker. The middle blocker works at the net, reads the opposing attack and reacts in a fraction of a second to close the block, so timing and anticipation matter as much as height.

Highlights

  • With my sub-17 team, we finished as the second best team in Portugal.
  • At 17 I played for my school. Our club had a collaboration with the school, and many of my teammates studied at the same private school.
  • That year we were the champions of the school competitions.
  • We then went to an international tournament representing Portugal. Thirty two countries took part, and we finished in 9th place even though we were one of the shortest teams.

What volleyball taught me

Playing as a middle blocker taught me to read a situation quickly, commit to a decision and recover immediately after a mistake. Finishing 9th among 32 countries with one of the shortest teams showed me how far preparation, timing and trust in teammates can take a group. I bring the same habits to my work: staying calm under pressure, communicating clearly and doing my part so the whole team succeeds.
